# 🏀 Steph-Inspired Joint-Friendly Strength Plan

**Frequency:** 3× per week  
**Duration:** ~30–35 minutes  
**Equipment:** Dumbbells, chair/box  
**Notes:** Knee supports ON for squats. Vertigo-safe (no floor work).

---

## Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- March in place – 60 sec  
- Standing hip hinges – 10 reps  
- Arm swings (front/back) – 20 reps  
- Shoulder circles – 10 each direction  
- Bodyweight sit-to-stand – 8 reps  

---

## Main Workout

### 1️⃣ Box Goblet Squat  
**3 sets × 8–10 reps**  
- Sit back to box/chair  
- 1-second pause on box  
- Shins mostly vertical  

---

### 2️⃣ Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (RDL)  
**3 sets × 10–12 reps**  
- Soft knees  
- Hinge at hips  
- Neutral spine & head  

---

### 3️⃣ Standing Y-Raise (Thumbs Up)  
**3 sets × 10–12 reps**  
- Light dumbbells  
- Raise in a Y shape  
- Stop below shoulder height  

---

### 4️⃣ Farmer Carry  
**3 sets × 30–45 seconds**  
- Walk tall  
- Short, controlled steps  
- Eyes forward  

---

### 5️⃣ Bicep Curls (Neutral / Hammer Grip)  
**3 sets × 10–12 reps**  
- Elbows close to sides  
- No swinging  
- 2–3 sec controlled lowering  

---

## Progression Rules
- Increase **reps OR weight**, not both  
- Progress only if knees feel good next day  
- Reduce range or load if knee discomfort appears  

---

## Optional Skill Finish (5 minutes)
- Stationary dribbling (low → high) – 60 sec  
- Crossovers in place – 60 sec  
- Figure-8 dribble (slow) – 60 sec  
- Rest 30 sec  
- Repeat once  

---

## Exercises to Avoid (For Now)
- Lunges  
- Jumping / plyometrics  
- Floor work (planks, push-ups)  
- Fast direction changes
